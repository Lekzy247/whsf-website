Platform

One system for agricultural discovery, learning and support.

AgriLearn AI combines crop knowledge, responsible AI, multilingual delivery and offline-aware design in a modular platform.

How it works

Users choose a location and crop, provide an image or question, and receive structured educational guidance and relevant resources.

AI modules

Crop diagnosis, food recognition, pest and disease learning, climate-smart lessons and conversational assistance share one workspace.

Data architecture

Crop records are stored as reusable structured data so new content appears automatically in search and filters.

Languages

The architecture supports English, French and Spanish first, with room for Arabic, Portuguese and African languages.

Offline awareness

Future mobile delivery will prioritize low-bandwidth lessons, cached crop guides and field-friendly interactions.

Privacy and safety

The platform minimizes data collection and never presents prototype output as a definitive diagnosis or treatment order.

Technology

Modular today, extensible tomorrow.

The current static application uses structured JSON, reusable components and progressive enhancement. Future phases can connect the same experience to APIs, computer vision models, weather services and an administrative content system.

Planned service layers

  1. Web and mobile interface
  2. Crop and location data services
  3. AI analysis and retrieval services
  4. Expert-reviewed knowledge base
  5. Impact, consent and governance controls
