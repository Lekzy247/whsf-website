What WHSF should watch closely

The automation should prioritize deadlines that create visibility, funding, travel, speaking or partnership opportunities for WHSF.

1
Registration deadline

Alert staff 30, 14 and 3 days before registration closes.

2
Call for speakers

Flag AI, STEM, digital inclusion, girls education, nonprofit tech and SDG speaking calls.

3
Grant or travel support

Prioritize events offering scholarships, sponsored attendance or NGO travel support.

4
UN/ECOSOC updates

Notify WHSF when new UN, ECOSOC, HLPF, SDG, youth or civil-society updates appear.

Official first, then technology ecosystem
