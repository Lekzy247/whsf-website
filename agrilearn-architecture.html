System design

AgriLearn AI Architecture

A modular, multilingual and offline-aware platform designed for responsible agricultural learning.

Mobile & Web

Farmer, student and extension-worker interfaces.

Language Layer

English, French and Spanish content delivery, with room for African languages.

AI Services

Crop image triage, food recognition, search and learning assistant.

Knowledge Base

Crop profiles, regional guides, safety notices and expert-reviewed learning content.

Regional Intelligence

Country, climate zone, season and crop-specific learning pathways.

Impact & Governance

Consent, audit trails, evaluation, safeguarding and transparent reporting.

Responsible AI controls

Human confirmation

Users are directed to qualified experts for treatment and high-risk decisions.

Confidence and limits

Outputs disclose uncertainty and distinguish education from diagnosis.

Privacy by design

Minimum necessary data, clear consent and retention controls.
