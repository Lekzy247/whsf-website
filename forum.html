Community rules

  1. Adults only, ID-verified. The forum is strictly for people aged 18 and over. Every member must pass an age check by a WHSF moderator using an official photo ID from their country (national ID card, passport, driver's licence, voter's card or residence permit) and a selfie holding the ID. Nobody can read or post until they are verified. The images are stored privately and deleted as soon as a decision is made. Using someone else's ID or a fake ID leads to a permanent ban. Do not share photos or details of children without their guardian's consent.
  2. Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or sexual content.
  3. No scams or money requests. WHSF does not disburse funds, loans, prizes or promotions to individuals and never asks for fees. Report any such post.
  4. Protect privacy. Don't post phone numbers, addresses or ID documents, yours or anyone else's.
  5. Verify before you trust. Anyone claiming to work for WHSF can be checked on the Verify staff page.
  6. Report problems. Use Report on a post. WHSF moderators can hide posts and suspend accounts.

See also our Child Safeguarding Policy and Privacy Policy.
