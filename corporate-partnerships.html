Corporate partnership hub

Turn corporate capability into measurable community opportunity.

WHSF works with companies that can contribute funding, technology, employee expertise, learning access and long-term programme support.

WHSF commitmentEvidence-led partnership design

Every proposal should define the people served, intended outcomes, responsibilities, safeguarding requirements and reporting schedule.

Partnership pathways

Four ways organisations can participate.

Funding

Programme sponsorship

Support defined cohorts, learning hubs, scholarships, equipment or multi-year programme delivery.

Technology

Devices and infrastructure

Provide appropriate devices, connectivity, cloud support, software or safe learning infrastructure.

People

Employee expertise

Contribute mentors, trainers, career speakers, evaluators or technical volunteers through a structured programme.

Innovation

Responsible pilots

Co-design responsible technology or AI pilots with clear safeguards, evaluation and community benefit.

Partnership process

From enquiry to accountable delivery.

1. Discover

Align priorities, geography, participants and available capabilities.

2. Design

Define scope, outcomes, budget, roles, safeguards and reporting.

3. Deliver

Launch with clear ownership, participant protection and communication.

4. Evidence

Report activities, outcomes, learning and agreed recognition.
