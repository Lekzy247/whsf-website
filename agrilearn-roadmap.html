Delivery roadmap

From co-design to responsible scale

A staged plan that validates usefulness, safety, language quality and measurable impact before expansion.

Phase 1

Discovery and expert review

Validate priority crops, regions, users, languages and safeguarding requirements.

Phase 2

Global prototype

Launch Crop Explorer, apple focus, pan-African crop library and trilingual interface.

Phase 3

Community pilots

Test with U.S. youth agriculture partners and institutions across North, West, Central, East and Southern Africa.

Phase 4

Evidence and model improvement

Evaluate learning gains, inclusion, usability, trust, safety and regional relevance.

Phase 5

Regional expansion

Add more crops, languages, expert networks, APIs and country-specific learning pathways.
