WHSF flagship digital education programme

Media Information Library (MIL)

Empowering Communities with Digital Knowledge, Responsible AI and Trusted Information

MIL equips children, young people, educators, women, community leaders, journalists and civil society organisations to access, evaluate, create and share trustworthy information in the digital age.

Explore the programmePartner with MIL

Knowledge for resilient communities

Learn critically. Create responsibly. Lead confidently.

The Media Information Library combines digital literacy, responsible artificial intelligence, media production, fact-checking, cybersecurity awareness, digital storytelling and community engagement.

Through inclusive, hands-on learning, participants gain the practical skills to navigate the information environment, use emerging technology ethically and create credible content that supports education, opportunity and positive social change.

Vision

Informed digital citizens

To build a generation of informed digital citizens who use technology responsibly, create trusted information and drive positive social change through innovation, education and community leadership.

Mission

Inclusive digital learning

To equip communities with media literacy, AI skills, critical thinking and creative communication while promoting ethical technology use, lifelong learning and sustainable development.

Standard

Built for world-class impact

MIL combines inclusive delivery, measurable outcomes, credible partnerships and real community projects in an award-ready model designed for global recognition and responsible scale.

Why MIL is different

A practical, inclusive and future-ready learning ecosystem.

MIL moves beyond traditional media literacy by combining technical knowledge, creative production, safety, leadership and entrepreneurship.
