# AgriLearn AI Phase 4

## Product goal
AgriLearn AI is an agricultural smart application that uses artificial intelligence to scan crops, interpret weather and environmental data, provide rapid educational diagnostics, deliver early warnings, and offer localized farming guidance that helps farmers prevent losses, use water and farm inputs more efficiently, and improve productivity.

## Implemented
- Smart Farming command center
- Weather and prevention module shell
- Regional early-alert demonstration
- Photo-diagnosis pathway
- Nutrient and soil learning assessment
- Localized advisory planner
- Yield-optimization checklist
- Versioned service contracts and safety gates

## Production integrations still required
- Verified weather and satellite provider
- Validated computer-vision model
- Regional pest and disease feeds
- Secure identity and farm profiles
- Cloud database, object storage and audit logs
- Agronomist-reviewed local treatment and fertilizer rules

Exact chemical or fertilizer recommendations remain disabled until those controls are in place.
