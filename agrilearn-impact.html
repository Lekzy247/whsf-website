Learning journey

From observation to safer action.

  1. Capture or describe a crop observation.
  2. Review an educational indication and confidence statement.
  3. Consult a qualified agricultural professional when needed.
  4. Record the outcome and complete a related micro-lesson.
  5. Use follow-up surveys to measure learning and adoption.

Measurement and transparency

AgriLearn AI will distinguish verified results from targets and projections. Proposed methods include attendance records, pre- and post-learning assessments, consent-based usage analytics, facilitator observations, participant surveys and independent pilot review.

  • No figure will be presented as achieved until supported by evidence.
  • Personal information will be minimized and protected.
  • Safety incidents, inaccurate guidance and complaints will be documented.
  • Results will be disaggregated by gender, age and participant group where appropriate and ethical.
