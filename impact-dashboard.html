Key indicators

WHSF impact at a glance.

These are reported administrative indicators, not independently audited outcome measures. Definitions, periods and current limitations are documented in the impact methodology.

Cumulative reported reach across technology education, STEM exposure, mentorship, school engagement and community activities.

12e-learning course tracks

Robotics, cybersecurity, drones, AI, STEM, eHealth, accessibility technology and more.

550+Young women reported supported

Participation in entrepreneurship, mentorship, networking and career-pathway activities; long-term follow-up is still being strengthened.

Certificates issued across recorded digital learning pathways; certificates are not presented as employment outcomes.
