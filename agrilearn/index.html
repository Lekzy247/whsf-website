Pest and disease guide

Start with the visible symptom and crop type.

Fall armyworm — maizeRagged holes, leaf damage and dark waste inside the whorl.

Remove heavily damaged leaves where practical, encourage natural predators and seek locally approved control advice before applying chemicals.

Cassava mosaic diseaseMottled leaves, distortion and reduced plant growth.

Remove affected plants, use disease-free cuttings and choose resistant varieties recommended for your area.

Tomato early blightBrown circular spots, often with ring patterns.

Remove infected lower leaves, improve airflow, avoid wetting foliage and rotate crops.

AphidsClusters of small insects, curling leaves and sticky residue.

Inspect new growth, encourage beneficial insects and use approved low-risk treatment where necessary.

Soil and nutrient guidance

Common signs can overlap, so soil testing is recommended.

Nitrogen

Older leaves may turn pale or yellow and growth can slow.

Action: Consider compost, well-managed manure or locally recommended nitrogen fertilizer.

Potassium

Leaf edges may yellow or brown, especially on older leaves.

Action: Use soil-test guidance and an appropriate potassium source.

Phosphorus

Plants may be stunted and leaves can become unusually dark or purple.

Action: Improve soil organic matter and apply phosphorus only at the recommended rate.

Soil health

Healthy soil holds water, supports roots and reduces crop stress.

Action: Add organic matter, reduce erosion and rotate crops.

About AgriSmart AI

A WHSF digital inclusion initiative.

AgriSmart AI is designed to help smallholder farmers, women, young people, extension workers and underserved communities use accessible digital tools for better agricultural decisions.

The project supports food security, climate resilience, responsible technology and inclusive livelihoods.
