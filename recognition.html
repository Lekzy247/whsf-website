Recognition & accreditation

Important claims, stated precisely.

This register separates organisational status, awards, formal partnerships and other recognition. A database listing, application or award does not imply endorsement of WHSF by the issuing institution.

Evidence standard

What every recognition entry should include.

Required record

  • Recognition or status name
  • Awarding or registering institution
  • Date and category
  • Official supporting link
  • Certificate, photograph or record reference

Required context

  • What the recognition means
  • What it does not mean
  • Whether it applies to WHSF or a programme
  • Expiry, renewal or reporting obligations
  • Permission for any displayed logo

Partner logo policy

WHSF should display a logo only with documented permission or a verifiable relationship. Formal programme partners, funders, training partners, event collaborators and external resource providers should be labelled separately.
