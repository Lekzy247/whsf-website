Platform capabilities

Designed for practical use in the field and classroom.

AgriLearn AI brings crop awareness, weather guidance, digital records and short agriculture lessons into one mobile-first experience.

Crop support

Image-assisted crop review

Users capture a clear crop-leaf image and receive an early indication of supported conditions, image-quality prompts and responsible next-step guidance.

Climate

Weather and field guidance

Simple local conditions can help users plan planting, irrigation, spraying and other activities.

Records

Digital field journal

Save observations, treatments, photos and activities to create a basic history for each farm or learning plot.

Learning

Micro-lessons

Short modules explain crop care, soil health, climate-smart practices, safe technology use and entrepreneurship.

Inclusion

Local-language access

Initial content is planned for English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo, using concise language and visual cues.

Connectivity

Offline-first design

Core learning and record functions are intended to remain useful where connectivity is limited or intermittent.

Planned user journey

Capture, understand, act and learn.

1. Capture

Take a clear image or enter a field observation.

2. Review

Receive easy-to-understand guidance and confidence information.

3. Confirm

Consult a qualified agricultural adviser before treatment decisions.

4. Record

Save the observation and follow-up actions.

5. Learn

Open a related lesson to strengthen future decision-making.

Development note: These are planned capabilities. Availability and scope will depend on prototype validation, funding, partnerships and field testing.
