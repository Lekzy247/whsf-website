WHSF Technology & Innovation Lab

A technology-for-impact platform connecting rural girls and young women to learning, innovation and opportunity.

WHSF uses practical technology to bridge education gaps, strengthen digital inclusion and prepare girls and young women for future-ready careers. The lab brings together devices, robotics, drones, AI, cybersecurity, assistive technology, sustainable tech and virtual learning into one mission-driven innovation ecosystem.

WHSF AI Assistant Suite

Practical AI tools for opportunity, learning and community resilience.

WHSF can start small with high-value AI assistants that help students, farmers, young women, volunteers and communities find trusted opportunities, avoid scams, access useful knowledge and communicate across local languages.

First public services to launch

Start with opportunity access, scam protection and trusted community information.

These assistants align strongly with WHSF’s digital literacy, Girls in ICT, STEM, agriculture technology, cybersecurity awareness, youth empowerment and safe online learning work.

01

AI Grant & Scholarship Finder

Finds scholarships, grants, fellowships, internships, STEM competitions and study-abroad opportunities relevant to girls, young women and WHSF learners.

Best for: students, ICT Girls Club, TechWomen, university applicants
02

AI Tech Opportunity Monitor

Tracks hackathons, technology conferences, women-in-tech programmes, AI training, cloud events, cybersecurity bootcamps and digital inclusion opportunities.

Best for: mentors, students, volunteers, programme coordinators
03

AI Agriculture Knowledge Assistant

Supports climate-smart agriculture learning with plain-language guidance on crop information, weather awareness, farm safety and verified agriculture resources.

Best for: rural girls, farmers, climate-smart agriculture projects
04

AI Health Information Verifier

Helps communities understand health information safely by encouraging trusted sources, prevention awareness and referral to qualified professionals for medical decisions.

Best for: parents, learners, community health awareness
05

AI Scam Detection Center

Flags suspicious scholarships, fake certificates, phishing links, false job offers, crypto scams, donation fraud and manipulated media for review and education.

Best for: online safety, cybersecurity training, donor protection
06

AI Tech News Curator

Summarizes useful technology news in AI, robotics, drones, cybersecurity, cloud, data centers and digital inclusion for WHSF learners and staff.

Best for: weekly learning updates and programme content
07

WhatsApp Information Assistant

Lets users ask simple questions such as “Is this scholarship real?” or “Where can I learn cybersecurity?” and receive guided next steps.

Best for: mobile-first communities and low-bandwidth access
08

Multilingual AI Translation

Supports local communities with simplified information in English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Pidgin English for wider inclusion.

Best for: rural outreach, parent communication, local-language learning

Mission-aligned sustainability

Technology that can support both impact and long-term funding.

Revenue should strengthen the mission. WHSF can build ethical earned-income pathways that help keep core access free for vulnerable learners.

  • Corporate-sponsored device drives, connectivity support and branded impact reports
  • Virtual partner rooms for donors, responsible brands, schools and technology companies
  • Professional training for organisations, with scholarships reserved for girls and young women
  • Certificate verification, advanced bootcamps, mentorship and project showcases
  • Grant-funded rural connectivity, solar learning kits and sustainable technology pilots
