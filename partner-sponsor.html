Sponsorship opportunities

Support programmes that create measurable impact.

Sponsors can support one learner, one classroom, one community hub or a full programme pathway. WHSF can align sponsorship with education, technology, women’s empowerment, digital inclusion or community resilience.

01

Sponsor a learner

Fund course access, learning materials, mentorship support, certificates and project review for students.

02

Sponsor a classroom

Support devices, internet access, teaching resources, learning hubs and safe digital learning spaces.

03

Sponsor a bootcamp

Help WHSF deliver AI, cybersecurity, robotics, drone technology, coding or digital safety training.

04

Sponsor an innovation challenge

Enable learners to build practical technology projects, pitch ideas and receive recognition.

05

Sponsor certificates

Support verifiable digital certificates that help learners show progress to schools, partners and employers.

06

Sponsor community outreach

Help WHSF reach rural and underserved communities with digital literacy and technology awareness.
