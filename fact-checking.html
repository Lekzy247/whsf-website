WHSF Verify

A non-political fact-checking platform for safer learning and community trust.

WHSF Verify helps young people, educators, farmers, parents and communities identify misinformation, prevent scams and make informed decisions through technology, digital literacy and responsible AI.

Submit a claim Explore verification areas Education • AI • Agriculture • Health • Cyber safety
Mission

Promoting truth, digital literacy and information integrity.

WHSF Verify is designed to protect learners, families, donors and rural communities from fake scholarships, scam opportunities, fraudulent certificates, fake aid campaigns, cyber fraud, manipulated media and misleading health, agriculture or technology claims.

Verification areas

WHSF Verify protects communities where misinformation causes real harm.

The platform focuses on practical, humanitarian and non-political verification that aligns with WHSF’s work in digital literacy, Girls in ICT, STEM, AI, cybersecurity, climate-smart agriculture, community development and safe online learning.

How WHSF Verify works

A simple verification pathway for learning, safety and trust.

WHSF Verify helps people ask better questions, check reliable sources and avoid spreading harmful or fraudulent information.

01

Submit claim

A visitor shares a claim, link, screenshot description, social media post or question that needs verification guidance.

02

AI-assisted triage

The system classifies the topic, highlights scam or safety signals, suggests source checks and prepares a review checklist.

03

Human review

WHSF reviewers compare official sources, institution websites, scientific information, trusted announcements, date context and media clues.

04

Clear verdict

The response can be marked verified, false, misleading or under review, with evidence and simple guidance for the public.

Media literacy

Skills learners can use every day.

WHSF helps learners build habits that reduce scams, false claims and unsafe sharing across education, technology, agriculture, health and humanitarian information.

🔎

Source checking

Teach students to identify original sources, official pages, fake accounts, misleading headlines and recycled content.

🖼️

Image and video clues

Use reverse image search habits, date checks, location clues and deepfake awareness to reduce harmful sharing.

🤖

Responsible AI use

Help learners understand AI hallucinations, prompt safety, citation habits and when human verification matters.

🛡️

Scam awareness

Protect communities from fake jobs, fake scholarships, phishing links, donation fraud, impersonation and false opportunity announcements.
