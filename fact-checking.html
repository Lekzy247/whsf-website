WHSF Digital Trust

Fact-Checking Platform for safer digital communities.

WHSF uses supported digital platforms and responsible technology to help students, volunteers, partners and communities counter misinformation, disinformation, fake news and hate speech, especially where public-interest information, elections and democratic participation are affected.

Trust workflow

Claim → Evidence → Review → Guidance

The platform is designed for civic education, public-interest verification and safer online participation. It is non-partisan and does not replace legal, medical, electoral authority or emergency advice. Sensitive cases are routed to WHSF support or safeguarding channels.

Public-interest protection

Countering harmful information while protecting civic trust.

WHSF’s approach focuses on education, verification and harm reduction. The platform can support communities before, during and after elections by helping people check claims, understand sources and avoid spreading harmful or false information.

🗳️

Elections & democratic processes

Review claims about voting, civic participation, public institutions, candidates, policies, deadlines and democratic processes using non-partisan source checks.

⚠️

Disinformation & fake news

Identify coordinated falsehoods, fabricated stories, manipulated media, misleading screenshots and recycled claims that may confuse or harm communities.

🧭

Public-interest information

Prioritize information affecting education, health, safety, humanitarian response, public services, opportunities and community welfare.

🤝

Hate speech awareness

Support safe reporting and education around hateful, dehumanizing or violence-inciting content while protecting dignity and inclusion.

How it works

A simple verification pathway for students and communities.

WHSF can use this platform to teach practical fact-checking habits while building a trusted public help point for civic and community information.

01

Submit claim

A visitor shares a claim, link, screenshot description, social media post or question that needs verification guidance.

02

AI-assisted triage

The platform categorizes the topic, flags potential hate speech or public-interest risk, suggests source types and prepares a review checklist.

03

Human review

WHSF reviewers compare credible sources, official statements, electoral or public authority information, date context, image clues and misinformation patterns.

04

Public guidance

The outcome explains what is known, what is uncertain, what sources were reviewed and how the public can verify similar claims.

Submit for review

Ask WHSF to review a claim or digital information risk.

This early version prepares an email enquiry for WHSF. Later it can connect to Supabase, Airtable or a case-management dashboard for claim tracking, reviewer assignment, source logs, risk level, response status and public-interest reporting.

  • Election, civic participation or democratic-process misinformation
  • Hate speech, incitement, harmful rumours or identity-based attacks
  • Social media rumours affecting students, women, children or communities
  • Scholarship, job, conference or donation scams
  • False health, safety, education or technology claims
  • Fake certificates, fake WHSF pages or suspicious staff claims

Media literacy

Skills learners can use every day.

WHSF helps learners build habits that reduce harmful sharing and strengthen democratic, educational and community trust.

🔎

Source checking

Teach students to identify original sources, official pages, fake accounts, misleading headlines and recycled content.

🖼️

Image and video clues

Use reverse image search habits, date checks, location clues and deepfake awareness to reduce harmful sharing.

🤖

Responsible AI use

Help learners understand AI hallucinations, prompt safety, citation habits and when human verification matters.

🛡️

Scam and hate-speech awareness

Protect communities from fake jobs, fake scholarships, phishing links, donation fraud, impersonation and harmful hateful narratives.

Supported platforms and technologies

Technology stack for responsible fact-checking.

The production version can combine human review with safe, auditable technology workflows.

AI

AI triage and summarization

DB

Secure case database

Learning resources
