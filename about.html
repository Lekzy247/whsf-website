About WHSF

About Us

World Humanitarian Support Foundation is a technology-driven humanitarian nonprofit expanding hope, inclusion and opportunity through digital skills, AI awareness, STEM education and responsible innovation.

Our story

Building opportunity through responsible technology

WHSF was established in the United States in June 2015 as a registered California nonprofit organization and registered in Nigeria in 2021. Since then, our work has connected humanitarian purpose with practical technology education.

We use digital literacy, e-learning, artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, mentorship and climate-smart innovation to create clearer pathways into education, enterprise, work and community participation.
