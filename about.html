Our story

Building opportunity through responsible technology

WHSF was established in the United States in June 2015 as a registered California nonprofit organization and registered in Nigeria in 2021. We use digital literacy, artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, mentorship and climate-smart innovation to create clearer pathways into education, enterprise, work and community participation.

  1. E
    We empowerPractical skills build confidence and agency.
  2. R
    We innovate responsiblyTechnology should be ethical, useful and inclusive.
  3. I
    We includeWomen, girls, young people and underserved communities belong at the centre.
  4. M
    We measure impactEvidence, accountability and learning guide our work.

How we work

We combine accessible education, hands-on practice, mentorship and partnerships so that learning leads to meaningful change.
