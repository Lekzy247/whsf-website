Future project portfolio

Focused projects with clear practical value.

Each project starts small, is tested in a real learning environment and is measured against simple operational outcomes.

Functional prototype

AgriSmart Mobile Farming Platform

A mobile-first platform that helps farmers scan crops, record symptoms, receive pest, disease and nutrient-deficiency guidance, check weather, track farms, request services and connect with agricultural buyers and sellers.

  • Camera-based crop assessment workflow
  • Weather and farm-location tools
  • Agricultural products and service orders
  • Buyer matching and produce marketplace
Priority: FlagshipOpen app prototype
🤖
Concept development

Agricultural Robotics Transport Pilot

A compact field robot designed to move tools, seedlings and harvested produce across farm terrain, reducing physical strain and limiting crop damage.

  • Measure time saved per trip
  • Test safe load capacity
  • Compare crop-damage rates
  • Train youth in basic robotics
Priority: High
🚁
Pilot-ready

Drone Crop Monitoring

Introduce safe drone use for aerial observation, crop-health checks, plot mapping and training demonstrations.

  • Map demonstration plots
  • Capture seasonal imagery
  • Identify visible stress zones
Priority: High
💧
Field testing

Smart Irrigation Demonstration

Combine drip irrigation, simple moisture checks and practical scheduling to improve water-use efficiency.

  • Track water use
  • Compare irrigation methods
  • Teach maintenance skills
Priority: Active

Research pathway

Natural Resource Management Lab

Develop practical methods for soil fertility, crop diversification, water conservation and responsible land use.

  • Soil-health records
  • Compost trials
  • Crop-diversification plots
Priority: Medium

Farm Sensors and Data Hub

Use affordable sensors and simple dashboards to record moisture, temperature, rainfall and field observations.

  • Low-cost sensor kits
  • Weekly data reviews
  • Student-led analysis
Priority: Medium

Renewable Energy Farm Systems

Explore solar-powered irrigation, charging and learning-lab systems for lower-cost, resilient field operations.

  • Solar water pumping
  • Device charging station
  • Energy-use monitoring
