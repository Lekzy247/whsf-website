About AgriSmart AI

Technology that supports people, land and livelihoods.

AgriSmart AI connects agriculture, climate education and digital inclusion. The programme is designed to make modern farming knowledge easier to understand and more useful in everyday community life.

Our approach combines hands-on training, local knowledge, mentorship and simple digital tools. The goal is not to replace farmers' experience, but to strengthen decision-making and create new pathways for girls and young women.
