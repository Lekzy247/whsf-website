Programme overview

A practical pathway from learning to livelihood.

The initiative brings agriculture and technology together so girls and underserved learners can respond to climate challenges, improve food production and build confidence for education, enterprise and employment.

Participants learn through demonstrations, field activities, digital lessons, mentorship and community projects. Local knowledge remains central, while responsible technology strengthens planning, record keeping, communication and problem-solving.

Expected outcomes

Knowledge that creates confidence and practical opportunity.

Climate resilience: learners understand local risks and sustainable responses.

Digital confidence: participants use essential digital tools safely and productively.

Improved livelihoods: learners gain practical farming and enterprise foundations.

Leadership: girls develop teamwork, communication and community problem-solving skills.
