What impact looks like

Learning is documented through real tasks and visible outcomes.

Participants do not receive credit for attendance alone. They establish plots, record observations, maintain digital evidence, complete practical assessments and deliver a community project.

Field evidencePhotos, measurements and activity logs
Skills verificationPractical rubrics and facilitator review
Community benefitProjects that leave useful infrastructure or knowledge behind
