Flagship future project

Agricultural Robotics Transport System

A practical project focused on automating the movement of crops, tools and supplies across farm terrain.

The pilot will test a compact robotic transport platform that can support field logistics while introducing young people to agricultural engineering, automation and responsible technology use.

ProblemMoving farm inputs and harvests can be slow, physically demanding and damaging to crops.
SolutionTest a small, controlled robotic cart on defined routes within a demonstration farm.
LearningParticipants gain hands-on exposure to mechanics, sensors, navigation, safety and data collection.
DecisionUse pilot results to decide whether the system should be improved, expanded or redesigned.

Pilot process

Keep innovation disciplined and easy to manage.

Every AgriSmart innovation project follows the same six-step process.

01

Define

Describe one clear farm problem and the people affected.

02

Design

Create a small solution with clear safety and success criteria.

03

Test

Run the pilot in a controlled field environment.

04

Measure

Collect simple before-and-after evidence.

05

Review

Identify what worked, what failed and what should change.

06

Scale

Expand only when the evidence supports it.
