Field-first programme model

Every module must produce visible evidence of learning.

Participants do not receive credit for attendance alone. They must complete supervised tasks and submit proof of practical competence.

1

Brief

Understand the objective, safety rules, materials and success criteria.

2

Demonstrate

Watch a facilitator perform the task correctly in the field or digital lab.

3

Practise

Complete the task individually or in a small team with supervision.

4

Record

Capture measurements, photos, observations, costs and lessons learned.

5

Present

Explain the result, challenges and next steps to peers and mentors.

12-week practical cohort

From bare ground to harvest, sales and community impact.

The schedule can be adapted to local crops, climate, school calendars and available land.

Week 1Farm site assessment

Measure the plot, inspect drainage, identify water access, record GPS location and photograph the site.

Field output: Site map and assessment sheet
Week 2Soil testing and land preparation

Test texture and pH where possible, identify erosion risks, prepare beds and plan plot layout.

Field output: Soil record and prepared beds
Week 3Nursery and compost

Mix growing media, establish a nursery, sow seed, label varieties and begin a compost unit.

Field output: Functional nursery and compost pile
Week 4Transplanting and spacing

Harden seedlings, transplant correctly, apply mulch and record plant population.

Field output: Established demonstration plot
Week 5Water management

Install a simple drip or controlled watering system and compare water-use methods.

Field output: Working irrigation setup
Week 6Crop monitoring

Measure plant height, count leaves, record rainfall and maintain a weekly growth log.

Field output: Crop monitoring dataset
Week 7Pest and disease scouting

Inspect leaves and stems, identify symptoms, document findings and apply safe integrated controls.

Field output: Pest scouting report
Week 8Digital farm records

Enter field observations, inputs and expenses into a simple spreadsheet or mobile record.

Digital output: Farm expense and activity tracker
Week 9Applied technology

Use weather information, GPS, mobile photography and available sensors to support decisions.

Digital output: Farm technology observation report
Week 10Harvest and post-harvest

Harvest safely, weigh produce, grade quality, reduce losses and prepare products for sale.

Field output: Harvest and quality record
Week 11Market day

Package produce, create a flyer, set prices, sell or simulate sales and calculate profit or loss.

Business output: Sales and profit report
Week 12Community showcase

Present the plot, digital portfolio, business results and community solution to partners and families.

Final output: Team presentation and practical assessment

Participant field kit

Tools for doing the work safely and correctly.

Kits can be issued individually or shared by small teams depending on programme resources.

Agriculture kit

  • Protective gloves
  • Hand trowel and measuring tape
  • Seed packets and plant labels
  • Watering can or irrigation materials
  • Field notebook and pencil

Digital kit

  • Tablet, laptop or smartphone access
  • Power bank and charging support
  • Spreadsheet and reporting templates
  • QR-coded participant ID
  • Photo and video evidence folder

Shared station equipment

  • Weighing scale and pH test tools
  • Rain gauge and moisture sensor
  • Wheelbarrow and basic farm tools
  • First-aid and hygiene supplies
  • Projector or display where available
