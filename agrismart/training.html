Girls in Agriculture & Climate-Smart Technology

Practical training for farms, classrooms and underserved communities.

Build confidence through climate-smart agriculture, digital literacy, applied technology and enterprise skills. The pathway is designed for rural and peri-urban girls aged 12–24, schools, youth groups and community learners.

Learning pathway

Progress from foundation knowledge to practical community action.

Learners can complete the pathway in sequence or use selected modules for school clubs, bootcamps and community workshops.

01

Foundation

Climate, agriculture and personal confidence

Introduction to food systems, climate change, safety, teamwork, communication and the role of girls in sustainable agriculture.

  • Understanding climate and local agriculture
  • Health, safety and safeguarding
  • Confidence and leadership foundations
02

Digital inclusion

Essential digital skills

Hands-on digital literacy for learners with limited access to technology and online learning.

  • Computer and smartphone basics
  • Typing, files and digital organisation
  • Email and online communication
  • Internet search and information checking
  • Cyber safety, privacy and responsible use
  • Word processing, spreadsheets and presentations
  • Introduction to responsible AI
03

Farm practice

Climate-smart crop production

Practical crop skills that support resilient, resource-conscious and locally appropriate production.

  • Soil health and land preparation
  • Crop and seed selection
  • Water conservation and irrigation basics
  • Composting and organic inputs
  • Integrated pest management
  • Harvest and post-harvest care
Open the crop guide →
04

Applied technology

Digital agriculture and smart-farming concepts

Introduction to affordable tools that can improve observation, planning, records and farm decisions.

  • Digital farm records and simple data
  • Weather information and seasonal planning
  • GPS, mapping and location awareness
  • Sensor, drone and IoT awareness
  • Mobile agriculture services
  • Responsible AI for farming questions
05

Livelihoods

Agribusiness and digital entrepreneurship

Foundational business skills for turning agricultural knowledge into sustainable livelihood opportunities.

  • Identifying a local market need
  • Budgeting and cost records
  • Pricing and customer service
  • Branding and digital marketing
  • Mobile payments and financial safety
  • Cooperatives and teamwork
06

Application

Community project and showcase

Learners work in teams to apply their knowledge to a school, farm or community challenge and present their solution.

  • Define a practical problem
  • Plan and test a small solution
  • Record progress and lessons
  • Present results to peers and partners

Digital skills centre

Simple, useful technology skills that strengthen everyday opportunity.

The digital skills track supports agriculture learning while also preparing participants for education, entrepreneurship and future work.

Device confidence

Use smartphones and computers, manage files and practise safe device care.

Productivity tools

Create documents, simple budgets, farm records and presentations.

Communication

Use email, messaging and virtual collaboration responsibly.

Online safety

Recognise scams, protect personal data and build healthy digital habits.

AI literacy

Understand what AI can and cannot do, ask better questions and verify information.

Digital enterprise

Build basic online visibility, communicate with customers and explore digital markets.

Flexible delivery

Designed for different levels of access.

School and youth clubs

Weekly or term-based sessions supported by teachers, volunteers and mentors.

Community bootcamps

Intensive practical workshops delivered through community partners and local facilitators.

Mobile learning

Short lessons and downloadable resources designed for phones and low-bandwidth environments.

Demonstration farms and labs

Hands-on practice using crops, digital records, tools and locally relevant projects.
