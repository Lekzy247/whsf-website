Before you donate

Legal name: World Humanitarian Support Foundation.
EIN: 47-4252621.

IRS-derived public records list WHSF as a 501(c)(3) organisation, tax-exempt since March 2016, with U.S. donations tax deductible. Donors should retain their receipt and confirm treatment for their own jurisdiction and circumstances.

Receipts, restrictions and refunds

PayPal provides transaction confirmation. For a formal acknowledgement, restricted gift, bank transfer, in-kind contribution, refund question or donation correction, contact WHSF before or promptly after giving.

A formal donation refund policy should still be approved and published.

Fraud awareness

Use only donation links reached through worldhsfoundation.org. WHSF does not ask donors to send money to personal WhatsApp contacts, unofficial social-media accounts or unknown payment recipients.
