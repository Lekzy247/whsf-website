Frequently asked questions

Answers about AgriLearn AI.

Learn about the proposed platform, its intended users, responsible-use safeguards and pilot approach.

What is AgriLearn AI?

AgriLearn AI is a proposed WHSF educational initiative designed to help farmers and learners access practical agricultural guidance, crop-observation support and digital learning tools.

Is AgriLearn AI already operational?

No. It is currently presented as a concept and educational prototype. Any future pilot will be announced only after partners, safeguards and implementation plans are confirmed.

Does it diagnose crop diseases?

No. The proposed image-review feature would provide educational suggestions only. It would not replace an agronomist, extension officer, laboratory test or qualified agricultural professional.

Who is the platform intended for?

The initiative is intended for smallholder farmers, students, educators, cooperatives, extension workers and community-based agricultural programmes.

Will it work offline?

Offline and low-connectivity options are planned for evaluation during future design and pilot stages, but no final capability has been confirmed.

Which crops and languages will be supported?

Initial crops, regions and languages will depend on the selected pilot communities, available expertise and verified local learning content.

How will user data be protected?

Any pilot would require clear consent, data minimisation, secure handling, limited access and transparent explanations of how information is used.

How can an organisation participate?

Institutions may contact WHSF regarding research, funding, technical collaboration, training support or possible pilot participation.
