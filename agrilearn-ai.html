WHSF Technology Innovation

AgriLearn AI

A multilingual, mobile agriculture platform designed to help farmers and young learners identify crop problems, understand weather conditions and make informed climate-smart decisions.

Explore the platformJoin the pilot

Designed for the field

Practical digital support for farmers and learners.

AgriLearn AI is being developed for communities where internet access may be limited, while keeping the experience simple, accessible and useful.

01

Crop Health Support

Farmers can take a photograph of a maize leaf and receive an early indication of common crop conditions, together with clear next-step guidance.

02

Weather Guidance

Local weather information can support decisions about planting, spraying, irrigation and other important field activities.

03

Digital Field Journal

Users can record observations, treatments and field activities, creating a simple history that supports better farm planning.

04

Agriculture Learning

Short, practical lessons can help young people and farmers learn about crop care, climate-smart practices and sustainable agriculture.

Programme timeline

From community insight to responsible deployment.

1

Discovery and co-design

Consult farmers, educators, young people and agricultural specialists to define needs, risks and practical use cases.

2

Prototype development

Build the mobile experience, crop-scan workflow, local-language content and field-journal features.

3

Controlled field testing

Evaluate usability, model performance, user understanding and responsible guidance with selected participants.

4

Training and pilot launch

Train facilitators and participants, deploy to selected locations and monitor outcomes.

5

Evaluation and expansion

Use evidence and community feedback to improve the platform and assess wider West African deployment.

Pilot geography

Starting locally, designed for wider West African relevance.

Proposed initial focus: selected farming and learning communities in Nigeria, with future expansion shaped by evidence, partnerships and local agricultural priorities.

The pilot will prioritize communities where digital access, crop-health awareness and youth agriculture learning can create meaningful value.
