WHSF Technology Innovation

AgriLearn AI

An offline-first, multilingual mobile platform helping farmers and learners identify crop disease, understand weather conditions and make practical climate-smart decisions.

Explore the MVP Partner with WHSF

Mobile MVP

Practical AI for agriculture across West Africa.

AgriLearn AI is designed for low-connectivity environments and supports field learning, crop health monitoring and local-language access.

01

Snap & Scan

Use the phone camera and an on-device TensorFlow Lite model to screen maize leaves for rust, blight or healthy crop conditions.

02

Weather Intelligence

Combine location-aware weather information with simple farming guidance for planting, treatment and field planning.

03

Field Journal & Map

Record crop observations, treatments and field locations so farmers can build a useful history for each plot.

04

Local-Language Learning

Deliver accessible agriculture lessons and treatment information in English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo.

Solution architecture

Fast in the field. Connected when possible.

Flutter Mobile AppDashboard, camera, lessons, journal and map.
On-device AITFLite maize model supports offline crop screening.
Cloud ServicesFirebase synchronizes records when connectivity returns.
Context ServicesGPS and weather data improve local recommendations.

Delivery roadmap

Five phases from foundation to deployment.

1

Repository Structure

Create the Flutter application with dedicated assets, models, screens and services folders.

2

Dependencies

Add libraries for TFLite, camera access, Google Maps, Firebase, GPS and localization.

3

Core AI Service

Load the maize model locally, preprocess images, run inference and map results to crop-health labels.

4

Localization

Create translation resources and a language toggle for English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo.

5

Build & Release

Connect Firebase and weather secrets, validate the application and publish through the WHSF GitHub workflow.
