WHSF Technology Innovation

AgriLearn AI

A multilingual, mobile agriculture platform designed to help farmers and young learners identify crop problems, understand weather conditions and make informed climate-smart decisions.

View all featuresJoin the pilot

Platform features

Practical digital support for farmers and learners.

AgriLearn AI is planned for communities where internet access may be limited, with an experience designed to remain simple, accessible and useful.

01

Image-assisted crop review

Capture a clear crop-leaf image and receive an early indication of supported conditions, image-quality prompts and responsible next-step guidance.

02

Weather and field guidance

Use simple local conditions to support planning for planting, irrigation, spraying and other field activities.

03

Digital field journal

Save observations, treatments, photographs and activities to create a basic history for each farm or learning plot.

04

Agriculture micro-lessons

Access short learning modules on crop care, soil health, climate-smart practices, safe technology use and entrepreneurship.

05

Local-language access

Initial content is planned for English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo, using concise wording and visual guidance.

06

Offline-first design

Core learning and record functions are intended to remain useful where connectivity is limited or intermittent.

Planned user journey

Capture, review, confirm, record and learn.

1. Capture

Take a clear image or enter a field observation.

2. Review

Receive easy-to-understand guidance and confidence information.

3. Confirm

Consult a qualified agricultural adviser before treatment decisions.

4. Record and learn

Save the observation and open a related lesson for future decision-making.

Prototype demonstration

Proposed pilot

Starting locally, designed for wider West African relevance.

The proposed initial focus is selected farming and learning communities in Nigeria. Pilot locations, participant numbers and delivery dates will be confirmed with participating communities, agricultural institutions and funding partners.
