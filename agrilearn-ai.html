Responsible AI by design

Trust is part of the product.

AgriLearn is designed to explain limitations, protect user data, avoid unsupported certainty and keep people involved in high-impact decisions.

Transparent assistanceUsers are told when content is AI-assisted and when information should be verified.
Human oversightUncertain and high-impact cases can be referred to qualified local experts.
Privacy and dignityPersonal and farm information should be collected only when necessary and handled responsibly.
Inclusive accessVoice, keyboard access, readable layouts and low-bandwidth design reduce participation barriers.
