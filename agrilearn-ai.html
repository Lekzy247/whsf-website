WHSF flagship innovation

Agricultural learning, organized around the farmer.

AgriLearn AI is an agricultural smart application that uses artificial intelligence to scan crops, interpret weather and environmental data, provide rapid educational diagnostics, deliver early warnings, and offer localized farming guidance that helps farmers prevent losses, use water and farm inputs more efficiently, and improve productivity.

Built for responsible growth

Designed to scale from pilot to platform.

New crops are added through structured data rather than new navigation. This keeps the experience simple as the knowledge base expands across countries, languages and partners.

Responsible AI commitments

  • Human confirmation for field decisions
  • Visible uncertainty and limitations
  • Minimum necessary data collection
  • Regional and language review
  • Clear distinction between prototypes and verified services
