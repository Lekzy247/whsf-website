WHSF Technology Innovation

AgriLearn AI

A multilingual, mobile agriculture platform designed to help farmers and young learners identify crop problems, understand weather conditions and make informed climate-smart decisions.

Explore the platform Partner with WHSF

Designed for the field

Practical digital support for farmers and learners.

AgriLearn AI is being developed for communities where internet access may be limited, while keeping the experience simple, accessible and useful.

01

Crop Health Support

Farmers can take a photograph of a maize leaf and receive an early indication of common crop conditions, together with clear next-step guidance.

02

Weather Guidance

Local weather information can support decisions about planting, spraying, irrigation and other important field activities.

03

Digital Field Journal

Users can record observations, treatments and field activities, creating a simple history that supports better farm planning.

04

Agriculture Learning

Short, practical lessons can help young people and farmers learn about crop care, climate-smart practices and sustainable agriculture.

How it works

Simple assistance, even where connectivity is limited.

CaptureTake a clear photograph of a crop leaf using a mobile phone.
ReviewThe platform evaluates the image and presents an easy-to-understand result.
LearnReceive practical crop-care information and recommended next steps.
RecordSave observations and field activities for future reference.

Programme development

Building responsibly from pilot to wider community use.

WHSF is developing AgriLearn AI in stages so that farmers, educators and agricultural partners can help shape a safe, relevant and practical platform.

1

Community Research

Engage farmers, young people, educators and agricultural specialists to understand local needs and priorities.

2

Platform Development

Create an accessible mobile experience for crop support, weather information, learning and field records.

3

Field Testing

Test the platform with selected communities and gather feedback on accuracy, clarity and usefulness.

4

Training & Local Languages

Prepare learning materials and expand access through English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo.

5

Pilot Deployment

Launch a monitored pilot with agricultural institutions, schools, cooperatives and community partners.

Community value

What AgriLearn AI aims to achieve.

Earlier Crop Awareness

Help farmers notice possible crop problems sooner and seek appropriate agricultural advice before losses increase.

Better-Informed Decisions

Make weather and field information easier to use when planning day-to-day farming activities.

Youth Skills Development

Introduce young people to agriculture, responsible AI, sustainability and practical digital problem-solving.

More Inclusive Access

Provide information in familiar languages and design for communities with limited or inconsistent connectivity.

Inclusive learning

Built for local-language access.

AgriLearn AI aims to make important crop-health information and agriculture learning easier to understand for farmers, girls, young people and community learners.

EnglishHausaYorubaIgbo

Clear and practical information

The platform will use simple labels, visual guidance and concise instructions rather than technical language. This helps users understand what they are seeing and what action they may need to take next.

AgriLearn AI is intended to support learning and early awareness. It does not replace professional agricultural diagnosis or advice.

Programme status

AgriLearn AI is currently in MVP development.

WHSF welcomes agricultural institutions, AI researchers, funders, educators, cooperatives and field partners interested in responsible testing, training and pilot deployment.
