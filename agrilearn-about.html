About the initiative

Responsible AI for stronger agricultural communities.

AgriLearn AI combines crop-health awareness, climate-smart learning, local-language access and youth digital skills in one practical mobile platform.

Explore the pilotView resources

Why it matters

Farmers need information that is timely, clear and locally relevant.

Smallholder farmers often face crop disease, changing weather patterns, limited extension support and inconsistent internet access. AgriLearn AI is designed to make useful knowledge easier to reach without presenting technology as a replacement for qualified agricultural expertise.

Mission

Expand access to practical agriculture knowledge through inclusive and responsible technology.

Vision

Communities where farmers and young people use digital tools confidently to improve learning, resilience and livelihoods.

Core principle

Human guidance, local context, safety and transparency remain central to every feature.

Responsible AI commitments

How WHSF intends to develop the platform.

Community co-design

Farmers, educators and agricultural specialists help shape requirements and language.

Clear limitations

Results are presented as guidance and early awareness, not definitive diagnosis.

Privacy by design

Collect only the information needed for learning, field records and programme evaluation.

Human escalation

Encourage users to contact qualified advisers before applying treatment.

Local-language access

Prioritize English, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo during initial development.

Evidence-led expansion

Scale only after usability, safety and impact are evaluated.
