Frequently asked questions

Useful next steps

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How can my organisation partner with WHSF?

Review the Corporate Partnerships page, then select Partnership in the form above and share your organisation, location and proposed support.

How can I volunteer or become a mentor?

Select Volunteering or Mentorship and tell us your skills, availability, location and preferred programme area.

How do I get help with a course or a certificate?

Select e-learning support or Certificate help. Include your student email, course name or certificate number and a short description of the issue.

How do I report suspected fraud?

Select Fraud / Scam report and share the suspicious account, message or link. Do not send money or disclose personal or financial information. You can also verify a WHSF employee by name and phone number.

Fraud awareness

Scammers have misused the WHSF name, logo and images to create fraudulent Facebook pages and WhatsApp groups. WHSF is not offering financial promotions. Please report suspicious activity to your local authorities.

Someone says they work for WHSF? Check their name and phone number against our staff records before you share any information or money.
