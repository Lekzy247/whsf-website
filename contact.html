Frequently asked questions

Useful next steps

How can my organisation partner with WHSF?

Review the Partner & Sponsor page, then select Partnership in the form above and share your organisation, location and proposed support.

How can I volunteer or become a mentor?

Select Volunteering or Mentorship and tell us your skills, availability, location and preferred programme area.

How do I get help with e-learning or a certificate?

Select e-learning support or Certificate help. Include your student email, course name or certificate number and a concise description of the issue.

How do I report suspected fraud?

Select Fraud / Scam report and share the suspicious account, message or link. Do not send money or disclose personal or financial information.
