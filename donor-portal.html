What happens next.

  1. PayPal opens in a separate secure page.
  2. You choose the donation amount and available giving frequency.
  3. PayPal processes payment and provides the transaction record.
  4. For receipt or allocation questions, contact WHSF with the transaction reference—never your password or full card number.

Fraud warning: WHSF does not offer financial promotions and will not request donations through an unknown personal WhatsApp account.
