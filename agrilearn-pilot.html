Proposed pilot programme

Testing AgriLearn AI responsibly with communities.

The proposed pilot will validate usability, safety, local-language content and practical value before wider deployment.

Community co-design

Work with farmers, educators, young people and agricultural specialists to refine needs and workflows.

Controlled testing

Evaluate crop-image guidance, learning modules, weather information and field-journal usability.

Training

Prepare facilitators and participants to use the platform safely and understand its limitations.

Monitoring

Track participation, learning, usability, accessibility and responsible-use indicators.

Status: This is a proposed pilot. Locations, participant numbers, dates and partners will be confirmed after approvals and funding.
