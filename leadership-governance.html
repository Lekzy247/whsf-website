Leadership & governance

Accountability should be visible.

WHSF is strengthening the public record of who governs the organisation, how decisions are overseen and which policies guide programme delivery.

Governance structure

Clear roles, responsible oversight.

Board of Directors

The board provides fiduciary, strategic and mission oversight. Verified names, officer roles, appointment terms and short biographies will be published after organisational confirmation.

Executive leadership

Executive leaders are responsible for strategy, operations, programme quality, partnerships and reporting. Verified profiles are being prepared for publication.

Programme leadership

Programme leads oversee delivery, safeguarding, learning quality, partner coordination and outcome monitoring.

Advisers and volunteers

Advisory roles do not replace board accountability. Public profiles will identify the nature and limits of each role.

Accountability commitments

Policies that support responsible delivery.

Safeguarding

Child and participant safety must guide programme design, communications, images, referrals and incident response.

Read safeguarding policy →

Privacy and data protection

WHSF aims to collect only necessary information, protect access and explain how participant and visitor data are used.

Read privacy policy →

Conflict of interest

Directors, leaders and decision-makers should disclose conflicts and recuse themselves where independence could be affected. A formal public statement is being prepared.

Responsible AI

Human oversight, privacy, accessibility, fairness, safety and clear limitations guide every AI-enabled initiative.
