Educational prototype

Preview the planned AgriLearn AI workflow.

This demonstration explains how users may capture a crop image, review guidance, confirm next steps and save a field record.

1. Capture

Take a clear photograph of a supported crop leaf using a mobile device.

2. Review

Receive an example result, image-quality prompt and clearly stated limitations.

3. Confirm

Consult a qualified agricultural adviser before treatment or major field decisions.

4. Record

Save the observation, date, field note and recommended follow-up.

Important: This website is an educational demonstration only. It does not run a production crop-disease model and does not provide a diagnosis.
